# Changelog | Seeds: A Store of Values

All notable changes to this publication are recorded here. Released editions are immutable and identified by their Git tag and commit SHA.

## 3.3.0 | 2026-10-06

- Seeds is set out as a root system: it records who is here and which of their work held up, and what a group builds later (projects, rewards for tasks, a map of what it knows, money) grows from that record and cannot rewrite it. New lines in the abstract, the introduction, *How a network starts* and the conclusion.
- New section, *Private by default*: no names on the network, and votes and challenges designed to be counted without revealing who cast them.
- The genesis set is called the first members rather than founders.
- 3.2 was an internal draft and was not published.

## 3.1.0 | 2026-10-05

- The companion specification is renamed *Seeds: Protocol Specification*. Reference and link updated. Content unchanged.

## 3.0.0 | 2026-10-05

- First public edition. Earlier versions were internal drafts. Describes the v2 rules of the reference runtime: joining mints nothing, no bond, no transfer, claims bound to the genesis hash, and a unit the protocol does not name.
