# Changelog | The Changing Shape of Polkadot

All notable changes to this publication are recorded here. Released editions are immutable and identified by their Git tag and commit SHA.

## 0.1.0 | 2026-10-19 (draft, unreleased)

- First draft. Reviews thirty Polkadot referenda from the founding of the Polkadot Community Foundation (730) to the strategy mandate (1954) and the onboarding operator confirmation (1955), with the register in `data/referenda.json`.
- Explains PCF units and what "bring their own funding" means.
- Written as a reference review, not a set of proposals. Section 8 reads each of Polkadot's pressure points against its counterpart in Birdbrain's own design.
- Release tag on publication: `publication/polkadot-changing-shape/v0.1.0`.
