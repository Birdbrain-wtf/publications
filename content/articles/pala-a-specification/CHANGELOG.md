# Changelog | Pala: a specification

All notable changes to this publication are recorded here. Released editions are immutable and identified by their Git tag and commit SHA.

## 0.2.0 | 2026-11-06 (draft)

- The whole novel was read, in six parts, and every citation checked against it. Section 9 no longer rests on a search, and its six gaps hold, each now noting where the text comes closest.
- Corrections: Murugan is already Raja, in his minority since 1954, not the heir. "No oil concessions to anyone" is spoken by an oil company's chairman. "Indispensable" describes cement and electrical equipment, not heavy industry. The 25% of royalties goes "to World Reconstruction" through the Crusade. "Nobody has more than three" children is reported as an outcome, not a rule, and contraceptives are "not compulsory". Initiation marks the passage out of childhood. The Privy Council's guardianship is an act reported by the Rani, not a stated power. Murugan's "just not interested" referred to consumer goods and was removed. The shots at the end are heard, not placed.
- The sequence of failure now covers Aldehyde's commission, the cable to London, the Palanese response to the known conspiracy, the astrological reason for bringing the action forward and the arrival before dawn.
- Additions: entry and visitor rules, health and pensions, science policy, the preventive measures against domination, the Raja of the Reform's precedent of ending a regency, Rendang's arms and claims, and the inducements offered.
- Release tag on publication: `publication/pala-a-specification/v0.2.0`.
